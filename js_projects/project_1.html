How I got this graph?

The linked video explains the system a lot better than I can, but the gist of it is that certain votes are more likely to come from traitors than faithfuls.

These votes come with point values to indicate how 'traitor-like' it is, and over time contestants will rack up a total, with the theory being that traitors display more behaviours that result in a higher total.

  • A 'burn vote', where a contestant votes for someone nobody else does: 0.47 points.
  • Voting for a traitor during the vote that they're banished: 0.33 points.
    • In addition, the contestant hadn't voted for that traitor previously: an additional 0.07 points.
  • On the other hand, not voting for a traitor during the vote they're banished: -1.28 (thus suggesting that traitors are unlikely to do this).
  • A banished traitor has voted for that player at a round table: -1.69 points.
  • A player is in the final group into breakfast with a traitor who is subsequently banished: -1.73 points.
