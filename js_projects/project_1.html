Hannah Fry's Traitors Point System

Source of the points system: Video link

How I got this graph?

The linked video explains the system a lot better than I can, but the gist of it is that certain votes are more likely to come from traitors than faithfuls.

These votes come with point values to indicate how 'traitor-like' it is, and over time contestants will rack up a total, with the theory being that traitors display more behaviours that result in a higher total:

  • A 'burn vote', where a contestant votes for someone nobody else does, carries a points value of 0.47.
  • Voting for a traitor during the vote that they're banished gives 0.33.
  • In addition, the contestant gains an additional 0.4 points if they hadn't voted for that traitor previously.
  • On the other hand, not voting for a traitor during the vote they're banished carries a point value of -1.28 (thus suggesting that traitors are unlikely to do this).
  • Players that a banished traitor voted for in the past lose 1.69 points.

    • In addition, Hannah suggests that being in the final group coming into breakfast with a banished traitor removes 1.73 points, but I did not implement this as it requires sorting more info, and there's a rumour that the producers film multiple breakfast takes to mix it up anyway, so in a scenario where you're attempting to use this in the castle yourself, this might not even be true.
